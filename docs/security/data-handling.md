# Data handling and security checklist

Before ingestion or use of real client material, confirm with the manager/client:

- Which sources and documents are approved, and what access/license terms apply?
- May content be sent to a cloud LLM, or must all processing remain on-premises?
- What data are confidential, personal, or otherwise restricted?
- How are source permissions enforced in indexing and retrieval?
- What needs to be logged for traceability, and what must never appear in logs?
- Who can review/approve drafts, and how are versions and corrections retained?
- How will backups, retention, deletion, vulnerability review, and access revocation work?

Do not treat this checklist as a formal compliance certification. Security, quality, and regulatory requirements must be confirmed by the responsible organization.
